# Verifying the 11-like boundary

The first boundary probes at two widely used page widths returned different ranks for the end of the eligible feed. Several other page widths agreed with one another. We selected 125 and 175 rows for the two full reconstructions before embedding any paper. The preliminary probes and their group IDs are retained alongside the census. Feed ranks include marked external posts. The atlas counts distinct research-paper groups after those posts are removed. [Dated boundary probes](../../results/2026-11-26-over-10/boundary-probes.json) · [Feed specification](https://api-dev.alphaxiv.org/api.json)

Each reconstruction collects every global page through a wholly ineligible page, records its score histogram, then independently recovers distinct group IDs using subject filters and additional page widths. Any reassignment to a different score uses an observation of that score for the same eligible group. Matching ID sets and complete capacity accounting at each score are required for verified coverage. The service supplies a changing feed rather than a transactional snapshot.
