# Learnings · 2026-11-26

The strict cutoff by rank included 20 externally marked posts. Checking each record’s type reconciled the feed rank and the paper count.

UMAP’s 23.49 percentage points (95% seed interval 23.36 to 23.61) gain measures local neighborhood fidelity. The lost-neighbor fraction motivates the separate recommendation list. At seed 42 the ten-PC ablation assigned 24.79% of papers, compared with 66.46% for ten-dimensional UMAP. Shared subject agreement among assigned and tagged pairs was 68.66% and 70.36%, respectively. These are conditional comparisons because the assigned sets can differ. The evaluation uses the frozen corpus and held-out tags.

The precision audit found a change in the tenth neighbor for 1 of 3,703 papers. The released recommendations rank the archived vectors in float64. [Boundary evidence and correction](../../reviews/precision.md)
