# Learnings · 2026-11-26-over-20

The strict cutoff by rank included 53 externally marked posts. Checking each record’s type reconciled the feed rank and the paper count.

UMAP’s 12.32 percentage points (95% seed interval 12.27 to 12.37) gain measures local neighborhood fidelity. The lost-neighbor fraction motivates the separate recommendation list. At seed 42 the ten-PC ablation assigned 13.35% of papers, compared with 61.99% for ten-dimensional UMAP. Shared subject agreement among assigned and tagged pairs was 37.19% and 79.95%, respectively. These are conditional comparisons because the assigned sets can differ. The evaluation uses the frozen corpus and held-out tags.

Both editions used 200 UMAP epochs per fit. The larger corpus changes each paper’s potential neighbors, and the two maps have independent coordinate systems.

The unfiltered passes skipped 7,502 and 8,154 distinct qualifying groups, including external entries, through overlap within tied vote scores. Checking the cutoff rank alone would have missed them.
